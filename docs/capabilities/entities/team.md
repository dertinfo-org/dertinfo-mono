# Entity: Team

## Description

A subset of a [group](group.md), formed to enter team activities and competitions. A team is often around five dancers. Members are not permanently assigned to a team in the platform. A team enters an event only through a [registration](registration.md).

## What can be done

- **Create** — group-admin, via [Create and manage group](../features/web/groups-manage.md).
- **Update** — group-admin. Change the team name, bio, and other team information.
- **Attach images** — group-admin. See [Image](image.md) and [Image handling](../system/image-handling.md).
- **Show or hide in the showcase** — group-admin.
- **Delete** — group-admin.
- **Enter an event** — group-admin, by including the team on a [registration](registration.md) for one or more [team activities](activity.md).

## States

This entity has no lifecycle states.

**Showcase** is a setting: shown, or hidden.
